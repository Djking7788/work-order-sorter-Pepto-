Use one work order per line. The sorter checks the entire line, so your line can contain a work-order number, name/description, and domain in any order.

PS E&I Domain contains HBSG-128-999-001
PEPTO Anything that does not meet the E&I PM or PS E&I criteria. E&I keyword rules take priority over domain rules.
